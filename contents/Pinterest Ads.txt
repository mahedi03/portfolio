Why Pinterest Ads Matter
Pinterest users are often in a planning stage, searching for ideas before they decide what to buy, wear, cook, or build. Ignoring this stage means a competitor's pin shows up in that search instead of yours, and a well-performing pin can keep driving traffic for weeks after the campaign launches, unlike most short-lived social posts. The main risk of delaying is losing visibility during the exact window when a Pinterest user is deciding what to purchase, which is harder to recover once a competitor's product becomes the saved reference point.
A common misunderstanding is treating Pinterest like Instagram or Facebook and reusing the same square creative and caption style. Pinterest ads perform better with vertical images, clear text overlay, and a search-friendly title and description, since Pinterest functions closer to a visual search engine than a social feed.

If you expect your ad spend to grow significantly, the service-charge model scales the management fee down as a percentage the more you spend, so it can work out more cost-effective at higher budgets.
