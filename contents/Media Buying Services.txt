Why Media Buying Matters
Running ads without a coordinated media buying strategy usually means budget waste — not from bad creative, but from poor platform selection and manual bid management that never adjusts fast enough. Two campaigns targeting the same audience on two platforms will often bid against each other without anyone noticing.
Professional media buying prevents this by treating each platform's auction system differently. Google's Quality Score, Meta's ad auction, and TikTok's algorithm each reward different signals, and a bid strategy built for one rarely performs well on another. Coordinated management means the budget moves toward whichever platform is producing results that period, instead of staying fixed on a split set months earlier.
One common misunderstanding is treating "boosting a post" or running a single Google campaign as media buying. Those are individual tactics. Media buying is the coordination layer above them — deciding which platforms get budget, how much, and why, based on where your audience actually is in their decision.
Delaying professional media buying usually means continued budget waste from manual bid management and duplicate audience targeting across platforms. Coordinated media buying prevents overlap, lowers cost per result over time, and gives a single view of which platform is actually driving revenue.

FAQs
What's the difference between media buying and media planning?
Media planning is the strategy — deciding which platforms, audiences, and budget splits make sense for your goals. Media buying is the execution — setting up, launching, and managing the actual ad purchases. Riha Web Tech handles both as one connected process rather than treating them as separate services.
Do I need to run ads on all six platforms, or can I start with one?
No. Most clients start with one or two platforms that match their audience and funnel stage, then expand once those campaigns are stable. Running all six from day one rarely makes sense on a smaller budget.
How is ChatGPT Ads different from traditional search or social ads?
ChatGPT Ads place your brand within conversational, answer-based interactions rather than a search results page or social feed. It's an early-stage channel with evolving formats, so it's best approached as a test-and-learn placement alongside your established channels, not a primary budget driver yet.
