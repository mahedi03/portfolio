7. Why This Service Matters
Google Ads gives a business visibility the moment a campaign goes live — someone searching "Google Ads agency in Dhaka" today can see your ad. That's the main advantage over SEO, which typically takes several months to build organic rankings for competitive terms.
The risk isn't the platform — it's running it without the basics in place. Two mistakes account for most wasted ad spend we see in new accounts: broad match keywords with no negative keyword list, and no conversion tracking, so the account has no way to learn what's actually working.
Compared to Facebook Ads, Google Ads reaches people who are already looking for a solution, not scrolling past one. Facebook Ads can work well for awareness and browsing-stage buyers; Google Ads tends to work better when someone already knows they want to buy or hire, and just needs to find the right provider.
None of this means Google Ads works for every business. If your product has very low search volume, or your website can't convert visitors once they arrive, fixing those issues usually comes before running ads — not after.

9. Our Process
Riha Web Tech's Google Ads process runs through five steps — audit, research, build, launch, and ongoing optimization — with conversion tracking confirmed before any campaign goes live.
1. Account audit or fresh setup. If you already have a Google Ads account, we review its history and structure first. If you're starting new, we set up the account and confirm access and billing.
2. Keyword and competitor research. We research what your potential customers actually search for, in both Bangla and English, and review what competitors are bidding on.

4. Launch and initial monitoring. Campaigns go live, and we watch performance closely during the first few weeks while Google's algorithm gathers data.
5. Ongoing optimization and reporting. We adjust bids, keywords, and ad copy based on real performance data, and send monthly reports showing what changed and why.

10. Pricing
Google Ads management cost in Bangladesh depends on two separate factors: the ad spend paid directly to Google, and a management fee that varies by account complexity and monthly budget.
These are not the same cost. Ad spend goes to Google to actually run your ads. The management fee covers the strategy, setup, and ongoing optimization work.
Pricing typically depends on:
* Number of active campaigns and ad groups
* Monthly ad spend budget
* How competitive your industry is (real estate and legal tend to cost more per click than local services)
* Whether conversion tracking or a landing page needs to be built from scratch
There's no single fixed number that applies to every business, since a small local service business and a national ecommerce store need very different account structures. Contact us with your budget range, and we'll tell you honestly whether it's enough to work with, and what a realistic starting scope looks like.

12. Timeline / Duration
Google Ads campaigns from Riha Web Tech typically launch within a few business days of account access and conversion tracking being confirmed. After that, expect an initial two-to-four-week learning phase where Google's system gathers performance data — this is normal and not a sign of a problem.

What is Google Ads management?
Google Ads management is the ongoing setup, monitoring, and optimization of paid search campaigns on Google's advertising platform. It covers keyword research, ad creation, conversion tracking, and budget management, so a business gets measurable results from its ad spend instead of just traffic.
Do I need a website before running Google Ads?
Yes, in most cases. You need somewhere to send the traffic — a website, landing page, or at a minimum, a working contact form. If you don't have one yet, we can build it as a separate project before starting your campaigns.
Can you manage Google Ads for any industry?

Do you require a long-term contract?

What happens if my campaigns aren't performing?
We review performance regularly and flag underperforming keywords, ads, or campaigns for restructuring or pausing rather than letting them continue spending without results. You'll always know what's changing and why.
