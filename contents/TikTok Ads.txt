7. Why This Service Matters
Ads that look like traditional Facebook or Instagram ads typically underperform on TikTok. The platform's For You Page algorithm rewards content that feels native: shot vertically, fast-paced, and closer to what a creator would post than what a brand would produce. An ad that reads as an obvious ad usually gets skipped in the first two seconds, and TikTok's cost-per-result climbs when that happens repeatedly.
The other common failure point is tracking. Since Apple's iOS 14.5 App Tracking Transparency changes, a TikTok Pixel installed without proper Events API configuration loses a meaningful share of the conversion signal. That means the algorithm optimizes toward the wrong audience, and it becomes hard to tell which creative or targeting choice is actually driving results, as opposed to being credited for a sale it didn't influence.
Neither problem is really about "TikTok being hard." Both come down to treating TikTok like a smaller version of Meta Ads instead of its own platform with its own rules.

14. Frequently Asked Questions
What is TikTok Ads management?
TikTok Ads management is the ongoing service of planning, building, launching, and optimizing paid ad campaigns on TikTok's Ads Manager platform. It covers strategy, creative production, audience targeting, tracking setup, and performance optimization: the full path from idea to running campaign.
How much does TikTok Ads management cost?
We offer two options: a service charge that scales with your ad spend (20% at lower budgets, down to 10% at $1,000+ monthly spend), or a fixed monthly package starting at ৳5,000. Ad spend paid to TikTok is always separate from the management fee in both models.
Do I need existing video content to run TikTok Ads?
No. While having existing footage or an active creator relationship speeds up launch, creative production can be built from scratch as part of the service, using product photography, short clips, or new filming depending on what's available.
How is TikTok Ads different from just boosting a post?
Boosting a post extends the reach of one piece of content with limited targeting control. TikTok Ads Manager campaigns allow specific objectives (traffic, conversions, app installs), detailed audience targeting, A/B testing across creative variations, and conversion tracking through the Pixel, none of which boosting supports.
What budget do I need to start TikTok Ads?
Starting budgets vary by industry and goal, and TikTok's learning phase performs better with consistent daily spend rather than a very small, inconsistent budget. Your free account audit will include a specific recommendation based on your goals and typical costs in your industry.
How long before I see results?
Most campaigns need one to two weeks in TikTok's learning phase before data is reliable enough to optimize toward stronger results. Meaningful, stable performance trends usually take three to four weeks to establish, though early signals (click-through rate, engagement) are visible sooner.
