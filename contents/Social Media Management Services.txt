6. What We Offer — Platform-Specific Management Services
Social media management isn't one skill applied four times — each platform has its own audience behavior, content format, and algorithm logic. So Riha Webtech has a specific platform-based social media manager. We manage four platforms as distinct services, each with its own calendar, posting cadence, and response process, rather than treating every channel the same way.
Below is what each platform-specific service includes and who it's actually built for.
6.1 Facebook Management
Facebook management covers page setup and optimization, a structured content calendar, daily posting, and comment/Messenger response for businesses that rely on Facebook as their primary customer touchpoint. It's best for businesses in Bangladesh and similar markets where Facebook remains the first place customers check before buying, and where Messenger inquiries often lead directly to sales. This service handles organic presence — page activity, community response, and content consistency — while paid Facebook advertising is managed as a separate service, so budgets and strategy stay clearly separated. A common mistake businesses make is posting consistently but never responding to comments or Messenger inquiries, which quietly costs more sales than inconsistent posting does.
6.2 Instagram Management
Instagram management creates a visual-first content calendar — including Reels, carousels, and Stories — with caption and hashtag strategies tailored to the platform's discovery algorithm. It's best suited for fashion, food, lifestyle, and e-commerce brands where product photography and short video drive most engagement. Unlike Facebook, Instagram rewards visual consistency and short-form video over link-outs and text-heavy posts, which means content built for Facebook often performs differently here without reformatting. Brands without an existing visual asset library should budget for photo or video content creation alongside this service, since captions alone won't carry a weak visual feed.
6.3 Pinterest Management
Pinterest management creates SEO-optimized pins and organizes boards around a brand's products or content categories, aimed at capturing search-driven, purchase-intent traffic rather than social engagement in the traditional sense. It's best for e-commerce, home goods, fashion, and content-driven brands where customers actively search for ideas before buying. Pinterest functions closer to a visual search engine than a social feed, so pin descriptions and keyword targeting matter more here than caption tone or posting frequency. Growth on Pinterest is typically slower and steadier than Facebook or Instagram, and this service isn't the right fit for businesses expecting fast follower growth or viral-style reach. Pinterest is one of the go to option for the e-commerce brand in usa, slowly but steadily also growing in Bangladesh. 
6.4 LinkedIn Management
LinkedIn management handles company page activity, thought-leadership post support, and coordination with employee advocacy efforts to build professional credibility rather than consumer-facing reach. It's built for B2B companies, agencies, consultants, and service providers whose buyers research a company's expertise before reaching out. Content here favors industry insight and company updates over promotional posts, since LinkedIn audiences respond to credibility signals differently than consumer platforms do. This service is not a fit for consumer retail or e-commerce brands targeting individual shoppers — those audiences are better served through Facebook, Instagram, or Pinterest instead. LinkedIn can be the go-to platform for those who sell services. Most of the leads from LinkedIn are direct sales-driven customers. So growing LinkedIn is a must for service providers.
7. Why Social Media Management Matters
Inconsistent posting doesn't just look unprofessional — it triggers a measurable drop in reach as platform algorithms deprioritize accounts that go quiet or post erratically. A bigger and more common issue than inconsistency, though, is platform mismatch: a B2B service business investing effort into Instagram while ignoring LinkedIn, or a retail brand posting company updates on LinkedIn while missing the visual audience actively browsing Instagram and Pinterest.
There's also frequent confusion between social media management and social media marketing. Management is the ongoing, organic work of keeping pages active, on-brand, and responsive — content, posting, and community response. Marketing typically includes paid advertising layered on top. Businesses that only need one but buy the other end up either overpaying for ads they didn't want or wondering why organic posting alone isn't generating the leads that paid campaigns were built for.

14. FAQs
What's the difference between social media management and social media marketing?
Social media management covers the ongoing organic work of posting content, maintaining a content calendar, and responding to comments and messages. Social media marketing typically adds paid advertising on top of that organic activity. Many businesses need both, but they're scoped and priced as separate services.
Which platform is right for my business?
It depends on the audience and buying behavior. Facebook and Instagram suit most consumer-facing Bangladeshi businesses, Pinterest fits e-commerce and product-discovery brands, and LinkedIn works best for B2B companies and service providers. The initial audit identifies the right platform mix before any content plan is built.
Do you create content or just post it?
Both. Content creation — captions, graphics, and coordination with the video team — is included as part of the platform-specific calendar, not billed as a separate add-on unless a project needs custom photography or video production.
Can you manage Bangla and English content together?
Yes. Captions, comments, and Messenger responses are handled in Bangla, English, or a mix, depending on how a business's actual customers communicate.
How is this different from just boosting posts myself?
Boosting individual posts is a small part of paid advertising, not management. Social media management includes the full content calendar, daily posting, and community response — the ongoing work that determines whether a boosted post has an active, responsive page behind it or not.
Can you manage social media alongside our existing paid ad campaigns?
Yes. Since Riha Web Tech also runs paid advertising on Meta, TikTok, and Google, organic management and paid campaigns can be coordinated to ensure messaging stays consistent across all.
