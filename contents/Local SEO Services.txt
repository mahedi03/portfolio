8. Why Local SEO Matters for Bangladeshi Businesses 
Delaying local SEO means competitors currently occupying the Google Local Pack keep collecting the calls, direction requests, and walk-ins that would otherwise come to you. In many local categories, once a competitor establishes strong reviews and citation consistency, it becomes progressively harder — and slower — to overtake them, which makes early action worth more than the same effort applied later.
Mobile search dominates how people in Bangladesh look for nearby businesses, and a growing share of that behavior now includes AI assistants like ChatGPT and Gemini surfacing local recommendations directly, not just traditional Google results. A common misunderstanding is that having an active Facebook Business Page is sufficient for local visibility — Facebook and Google Maps are ranked separately, and Google's Local Pack draws primarily from your Google Business Profile, website content, and citation consistency, not your social presence alone.

15. FAQs 
Is a Facebook Business Page enough for local SEO in Bangladesh? 
No. A Facebook Business Page and Google Business Profile are ranked separately. Facebook activity can support brand visibility and reviews, but Google's Local Pack relies primarily on your Google Business Profile, website content, and citation consistency — so both need attention, not just one.
Do I need a website to rank in Google Maps? 
A website isn't always required to appear on Google Maps, since a Google Business Profile can rank on its own. However, a website with local content and schema markup significantly strengthens your ranking signals and gives customers a place to learn more before contacting you.
How is local SEO different from regular SEO? 
Local SEO targets geographic, "near me," and city-specific searches tied to a Google Business Profile and physical service area, while general SEO focuses on broader, non-location-based search visibility. Businesses with a storefront or defined service area typically need local SEO first.
Can you help a business with multiple branches in different cities? 
Yes. Multi-location businesses need a separate Google Business Profile and citation strategy for each branch, along with location-specific content on the website, and pricing is scoped based on the number of locations involved.
How long before I see results from local SEO? 
Most businesses see initial Local Pack movement within 60–90 days, with stronger results building over 4–6 months, depending on how established your existing profile and reviews already are.
Will I have to wait months to know if this is working? 
No. You'll get a monthly report from month one showing GBP activity, citation progress, and early ranking movement — so you can see the work happening even before the Local Pack shifts noticeably, which typically takes 60–90 days.
