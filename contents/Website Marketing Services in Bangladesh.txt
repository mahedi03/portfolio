Answer Engine Optimization (AEO)
Search behavior has shifted. A growing share of queries are now answered directly by AI tools — ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Claude, Grok, DeepSeek — without the user ever clicking a link. AEO is the practice of structuring and writing your content so it gets cited, quoted, and surfaced by these systems.
We optimize your content with direct answer blocks, definition sections, structured FAQ formats, retrieval-ready language, and entity relationship depth — the specific signals AI answer engines use to decide what to cite. If your competitors are ranking on Google but invisible to AI search, this is the gap worth closing.
Generative Engine Optimization (GEO)
GEO takes AEO a step further. Where AEO focuses on getting your content cited in AI-generated answers, GEO focuses on building your brand's presence as a trusted entity across the generative web — the ecosystem of large language models, AI-powered search results, and knowledge graph systems that increasingly shape what people find and trust.
We help your business become the answer, not just a result. That means topical authority building, entity-based content structuring, semantic coverage depth, and making sure your brand, services, and expertise are represented consistently across the sources AI systems draw from.
Local SEO 
For businesses that serve a specific city, region, or service area, visibility in local search results is often more valuable than broad national rankings. Local SEO focuses on the signals that determine whether your business appears when someone nearby searches for what you offer.
Our local SEO work includes location-based keyword research, on-page optimization for city and service-area pages, local citation building across relevant directories, and NAP (name, address, phone number) consistency across the web. We also address technical factors that affect local ranking — crawlability, page speed, mobile usability, and schema markup that helps Google understand your service area and business type.
Best suited for brick-and-mortar businesses, service-area businesses, and any company where the customer's location determines whether they're a viable lead.
Google My Business Profile
Your Google Business Profile is often the first thing a potential customer sees — before they visit your website, read a review, or make contact. A poorly managed profile loses clicks to competitors with more complete, active listings. A well-managed one drives calls, direction requests, and website visits directly from Google Search and Maps.
We handle full GBP optimization and ongoing management, including:
* Profile setup and category selection
* Business description written for both search visibility and reader conversion
* Service and product listings with search-optimized descriptions
* Photo and video uploads that improve engagement signals
* Q&A monitoring and response
* Regular Google Posts to keep the profile active
* Review response management
* Tracking of calls, direction requests, and profile views through GBP Insights
We also optimize for the Google Map Pack — the three-business block that appears at the top of local search results and captures the majority of local click traffic. Appearing there consistently requires a combination of profile quality, local SEO, and review authority, all of which we manage together as a connected strategy rather than separate tasks.

Link Building 
Earning backlinks through guest post placements, broken-link outreach, and content-based link acquisition — manual, relevance-based, and built around sites that have genuine editorial standards.
We don't buy links in bulk, participate in private blog networks, or use automated outreach tools that send 500 identical emails a day. Every link we build is placed on a relevant site, in an editorial context, using anchor text that makes sense to a human reader — which is what Google's systems are increasingly good at evaluating.
Google AdSense Approval Support 
If your site has been rejected for AdSense — or you want to get it right before applying — we review your site against Google's current AdSense policy requirements and fix the issues that most commonly cause rejection.
This includes content depth and originality assessment, required page review (privacy policy, about page, contact page), site structure and navigation clarity, ad placement planning that meets policy guidelines, and a check for any content categories AdSense restricts. We'll tell you honestly whether your site is ready, what needs to change, and how to change it before you apply.
One important note: final approval is always Google's decision. We prepare your site to meet their requirements as thoroughly as possible — we can't override their review process, and anyone who claims otherwise isn't being straight with you.
