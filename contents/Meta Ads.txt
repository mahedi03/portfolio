Included:

The three parts of Meta Ads management: every campaign comes down to three moving pieces working together. Audience targeting decides who sees the ad. Graphics and copy decide whether they stop scrolling and act. Media buying decides how efficiently the budget turns into results. Weak execution on any one of the three drags down the other two, no matter how strong they are individually.
Not included: ad spend itself, and raw video or photo shoots unless added as a separate creative production service. 
Best fit for: ecommerce brands running product catalogs, local service businesses that need booked appointments or calls, and B2B businesses running lead generation campaigns. Strategy and campaign structure differ across these three, and we build accordingly rather than using one template for all.
7. Why This Service Matters
An unmanaged or DIY Meta Ads account usually fails for one of three reasons: the Pixel and Conversion API were never configured correctly, the account never left the learning phase before the budget was pulled, or the same one or two ad creatives ran for months without testing. Since Apple's iOS 14 privacy changes, tracking has gotten harder, not easier, which means Meta's algorithm has less signal to optimize toward unless CAPI is set up properly as a backup data source. None of this means Meta Ads are unreliable. It means the setup work at the start determines almost everything that happens after.
There is also a trust dimension beyond direct response. An active, well-run Meta presence signals to potential customers that a business is real, current, and worth engaging with, especially for service businesses and B2B brands where a prospect will often check Facebook or Instagram before making contact. Ads that look thrown together undercut that trust even if the targeting behind them is sound.

11. Markets We Serve
We manage Meta Ads for clients across more than 20 countries, including North America, Europe, the Middle East, and South Asia.
Meta Ads in Bangladesh
For Bangladesh-based clients or anyone targeting Bangladeshi audiences, the platform landscape looks different from that in Western markets. As of early 2026, Bangladesh has roughly 72 million Facebook users, close to 40 percent of the population, making it the dominant social platform in the country. Instagram is far smaller by comparison, at around 10 million users, about 5.5 percent of the population, while Messenger sits close to Facebook at roughly 68.5 million users. Facebook's ad audience in the country skews heavily male, with men making up around 63 percent of the advertising audience, and the 18 to 24 age group represents the single largest user segment on both Facebook and Instagram.
This has direct targeting implications. A campaign built for a Western e-commerce brand, where Instagram often carries equal or greater weight, needs to be restructured for Bangladesh, where Facebook and Messenger carry most of the volume. Messenger based lead capture, common for local service businesses and small ecommerce sellers in Bangladesh, is a channel with far less relevance in markets like the US or UK. Mobile-only usage is also close to universal, so creative needs to be built mobile first rather than adapted from desktop formats after the fact.
We build strategy around these regional differences rather than applying one global targeting template across every market.

12. Timeline
Meta campaigns typically need one to two weeks of active spend per campaign before the algorithm has enough data to optimize properly. This is often called the learning phase, and pulling a budget or making major changes during this window usually resets progress rather than speeding it up.
What speeds results up: having creative ready before launch, correct Pixel and CAPI setup from day one, and a stable budget that is not paused or changed frequently.
What slows results down: incomplete tracking setup, frequent campaign edits during the learning phase, and creative that is not refreshed as it fatigues.

14. FAQs
How much should I spend on Meta Ads to start? 
Most businesses see enough signal to evaluate performance with a modest daily budget sustained over the one to two week learning phase. The right number depends on your average order value or deal size, which we help calculate during the free consultation.
Do you need access to my Facebook Business Manager? 
Yes, we need Business Manager access to configure the Pixel, Conversion API, and ad accounts correctly. Access is granted through Meta's own partner permission system, so you retain full ownership of your account at all times.
How is Meta Ads different from Google Ads? 
Google Ads targets people actively searching for something. Meta Ads targets people based on who they are and what they engage with, before they are actively searching. This makes Meta stronger for demand generation and Google stronger for capturing existing demand.
What if I have had a bad experience with an agency before? 
This is common, usually caused by poor tracking setup or a lack of reporting transparency. Our audit first process is built specifically to catch what a previous account may have missed before we build anything new.
Do you handle ad creative too? 
Yes, creative testing is part of the core service. If you need full video or photo production beyond ad copy and static design, that is scoped separately depending on volume.
