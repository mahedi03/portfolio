7. Why This Service Matters
ChatGPT now has a large weekly user base on its free tiers, and OpenAI has said it wants advertising revenue to grow substantially over the next few years. That means competition for attention inside ChatGPT will increase as more countries and categories become eligible.
Waiting has a real cost. Businesses that build their context-hint strategy, creative, and tracking setup in advance are able to launch as soon as their market opens or their account is approved. Businesses that wait until access opens are starting their strategy work from scratch, while competitors are already live.
There is also a common misunderstanding worth clearing up: ChatGPT Ads do not change what ChatGPT tells the user. OpenAI keeps sponsored placements clearly labeled and separate from the answer itself, and ads only appear to logged-in Free and Go tier users — paid ChatGPT subscribers do not see them. This matters for setting realistic expectations about reach.
